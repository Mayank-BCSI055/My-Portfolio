## 1\. Introduction

This document defines the target audience for the Personal Portfolio Website .

The purpose of this document is to identify the different types of users who may visit the website , understand their goals and expectations , and define how the website should serve each audience .

Understanding the target audience will help guide :

-   Website structure
    
-   Navigation
    
-   Content Organization
    
-   UI / UX Decisions
    
-   Project Presentation
    
-   Technical Information
    
-   Call - to - Action Placement
    
-   Resume Accessibility
    
-   Contact Functionality
    
-   Overall Communication Style
    

The website should primarily focus on professional visitors who want to quickly understand my technical background , capabilities , experience , and projects .

---

The website will have several categories of potential visitors .

Target Audience

│

├── Primary Audience

│      └── Technical Interviewers

│

├── Secondary Audience

## 4\. Primary Audience

The Primary Audience represents users who are most likely to influence professional opportunities .

The website should be designed primarily around their needs .

---

## 5\. Recruiters

### 5.1 Audience Description

Recruiters may visit the website while evaluating candidates for internships or software development positions .

They may not have extensive technical knowledge of every technology listed on the website .

Their primary goal is to quickly understand :

-   Who I am
    
-   What role I am interested in
    
-   What technologies I know
    
-   What projects I have built
    
-   What experience I have
    
-   How to contact me
    

### 5.2 Recruiter Goals

Recruiters should be able to :

-   Understand my professional identity
    
-   Identify my technical specialization
    
-   Review my experience
    
-   Find my resume
    
-   Review selected projects
    
-   Find contact information
    
-   Access professional profiles
    

### 5.3 Recruiter Information Requirements

Important Information should include :

-   Name
    
-   Professional Title
    
-   Short Introduction
    
-   Technical Skills
    
-   Experience
    
-   Projects
    
-   Education
    
-   Resume
    
-   Contact Information
    
-   GitHub
    
-   LinkedIn
    

### 5.4 Recruiter User Journey

Recruiter

     ↓

Home Page

     ↓

Professional Introduction

     ↓

Skills

     ↓

Experience

     ↓

Projects

     ↓

Resume

     ↓

Contact

### 5.5 Design Considerations

The website should allow recruiters to quickly find important information .

Therefore :

-   Navigation should be simple .
    
-   Resume should be easy to access .
    
-   Important Skills should be visible .
    
-   Projects should have concise summaries .
    
-   Contact Information should be easy to find .
    
-   The Website should avoid unnecessary technical complexity in the main presentation .

The website should provide :

-   Keyboard Navigation

-   Clear Headings

## 29\. Audience - Based Design Principles

The following principles will guide design decisions :

### 29.1 Principle 1 : Clarity First

Visitors should understand the purpose of the website quickly .

### 29.2 Principle 2 : Important Information First

Professional and Career - Related information should be easy to find .

### 29.3 Principle 3 : Progressive Detail

Show simple information first and provide deeper technical information when required .

### 29.4 Principle 4 : Minimal Friction

Visitors should not need many clicks to reach important information .

### 29.5 Principle 5 : Professional Presentation

The website should communicate professionalism without unnecessary visual complexity .

### 29.6 Principle 6 : Technical Evidence

Claims about technical ability should be supported by actual projects , repositories , and demonstrations where possible .

## 34\. Summary

The Personal Portfolio Website will primarily target recruiters , hiring managers , and technical interviewers .

These users need to quickly understand my professional identity , technical skills , experience , projects , and career capabilities .

Secondary audiences such as developers and potential clients will require additional information about projects , technologies , implementation details , and capabilities .

The website will therefore use a **progressive information strategy** :

Simple Overview

          ↓

Professional Information

          ↓

Project Details

          ↓

Technical Details

          ↓

Source Code / GitHub

This approach allows non - technical visitors to understand the portfolio without being overwhelmed while still providing enough technical depth for experienced developers and interviewers .

The primary audience principle is :

> **Make the important information easy to find , keep the initial presentation simple , and provide deeper technical information when the visitor needs it .**

The target audience definition will guide future decisions related to content , navigation , UI / UX , project presentation , SEO , accessibility , and functionality .
